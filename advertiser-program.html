About the Program

Our Advertiser & Partner Program is designed for individuals who can promote our products and bring genuine customers through WhatsApp, Facebook, phone calls, personal networks, face-to-face marketing and other approved channels.

Advertisers focus on marketing and sales. The company manages sale verification, order processing, collection in Damaturu and delivery for customers outside Damaturu, subject to applicable delivery coverage and terms.

Why Become an Advertiser?

💰 Earn Commission

Earn commission on eligible sales successfully attributed to you through the company's system.

📊 Track Performance

Use your advertiser dashboard to monitor customers, Sale Claims, completed sales and commission status.

🔐 Secure Verification

Each sale receives a unique one-time verification code generated by the company system.

🧾 Transparent Records

Every sale receives a unique Sale ID and is linked to your Advertiser ID.

📱 Promote Anywhere

Reach customers through WhatsApp, Facebook, phone calls, personal contacts and face-to-face marketing.

🚚 We Handle Delivery

For customers outside Damaturu, the company arranges delivery according to applicable coverage and terms.

How the Advertiser Program Works

Your job is to promote and sell. Our company handles the order and delivery process.

1. Register

Submit your advertiser application through the official website.

2. Get Approved

Your application is reviewed. You must be approved before operating as an active advertiser and creating Sale Claims.

3. Promote & Sell

Promote our products and find genuine customers through your network and approved channels.

4. Create a Sale Claim

Record the customer name, phone, product, quantity, agreed price, location, payment status and expected collection/delivery date.

5. Receive a One-Time Verification Code

The system generates a unique code for that specific customer/order. Advertisers do not create codes manually.

6. Give the Code to the Customer

The advertiser can send the customer's code through WhatsApp, phone or another appropriate communication channel.

7. Customer in Damaturu

The customer comes to the company's collection point, presents the valid code, and the company verifies the order before releasing the goods.

8. Customer Outside Damaturu

The customer does not need to travel to Damaturu. After proper verification, MK Abuzahra Global Ventures Ltd arranges delivery to the customer's location, subject to applicable delivery coverage and terms.

9. Commission

When the sale meets the company's eligibility requirements, the commission proceeds through the company's commission process.

PROMOTE → FIND CUSTOMER → SELL → CREATE SALE CLAIM → GIVE CUSTOMER CODE → COMPANY VERIFIES → COLLECTION OR DELIVERY → ELIGIBLE COMMISSION

What the Advertiser Does

PROMOTE → FIND CUSTOMER → SELL → CREATE SALE CLAIM → GIVE CUSTOMER THE CODE

The advertiser's primary responsibility is marketing and sales. The advertiser does not need to personally deliver orders to customers outside Damaturu.

What the Company Does

VERIFY → PROCESS ORDER → PREPARE GOODS → CUSTOMER COLLECTS IN DAMATURU OR COMPANY ARRANGES DELIVERY OUTSIDE DAMATURU

Commission

Advertisers earn commission according to the current commission rate set by MK Abuzahra Global Ventures Ltd.

Example: If a product sells for ₦16,000 and the applicable rate is 10%, the commission is ₦1,600.

The rate may change according to company policy, product, campaign or advertiser arrangement. Creating a Sale Claim does not automatically make a commission payable; the sale must be verified and satisfy the company's eligibility requirements.

Commission Status

PENDING → ELIGIBLE → PAID

Advertiser Responsibilities

  • Provide genuine customer information.
  • Promote company products honestly.
  • Provide accurate product and pricing information.
  • Create Sale Claims before the order is processed.
  • Give the correct verification code to the correct customer.
  • Represent the company professionally.
  • Follow company policies.
  • Never create or manipulate verification codes.
  • Never claim another advertiser's customer.
  • Never create fake, duplicate or fraudulent sales.

Fraudulent activity or serious policy violations may result in suspension or termination of an advertiser account.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need an account?

Yes. You must register and receive approval before operating as an active advertiser.

Can I advertise through WhatsApp?

Yes, subject to company policies.

Who creates the code?

The company system automatically generates the unique one-time verification code.

Can I create my own code?

No.

What if my customer is in Damaturu?

The customer can collect from the company after presenting a valid code and completing verification.

What if my customer is outside Damaturu?

The company can arrange delivery to the customer's location, subject to applicable delivery coverage and terms.

Does the advertiser deliver the goods?

No. The advertiser focuses on marketing and sales. The company handles order processing and delivery outside Damaturu.

When is commission paid?

Commission follows the company's eligibility and payment process.

Can my account be suspended?

Yes, for fraud, misconduct or serious violations of company policies.
